# Split Path ### Game Design Outline --- ## 1. Game Idea **Split Path** is a small, story-driven RPG about a little cube who gets reborn and has to complete challenges, make choices, and collect **Divine Points**. The game should feel like something that came from a sketchbook: - Simple characters - Weird ideas - Hand-drawn-looking UI - Lots of small details - Humor - Things that can change depending on what the player does The game starts simple, but the world and mechanics can become increasingly strange as the player progresses. --- # 2. Plot ## Plot 1 — The Beginning You play as a small little creature. For now, the creature is basically a small cube-like character. The player is introduced to the world and learns the controlls. There should be a feeling that something is slightly "off" about the world. The player can discover things that may change later. ### Important idea: The player should be able to make decisions that actually matter. --- ## Plot 2 — Gaining Points As the player purifies eneymys, they gain **Divine Points**. These points can be used for the divine path. The player begins discovering that Divine Points aren't just a normal currency. They are connected to the world itself. The player may eventually realize that the points are being used to change or become the form you were originally before you were reborn. --- ## Plot 3 — The World Changes The game begins changing based on what the player has done. For example: - the player can change their form. - Old areas may contain new things. The exact extent of these changes is still undecided. --- # 3. Story ## Story 1 — The Creature You play as a creature called a pristine cube taking restoring enemys and gaining divine points. The player doesn't necessarily understand everything that's happening, but lears some stuff from their first life. The player gradually learns about the world alongside the character. --- ## Story 2 — What Happened? At some point, the player learns that something was taken from them. Possibly their original "divine" power/status. The exact explanation is still being developed. The player now has to work their way back toward what they originally had. --- ## Story 3 — Getting Divine Points The player restores enemys to earn Divine Points. They can use their tools, items, and understanding of the challenges to progress. Eventually, the player should have enough Divine Points to begin upgrading themselfs. --- # 4. Gameplay The main gameplay loop is: 1. Find a enemy. 2. battle them. 3. restore them. 4. now that they are restored, talk to them. 5. gain divine points. 6. repeat the proccess. The important part is that the enemys are restored back into NPCs, and can populate villages and help the player rather then just giving divine points --- # 5. battles battles are the one of the most important part of the game. They can involve things such as: - attacking - healling - defending - Timing inputs ### Important: **NO BATTLE PASS! EVER!!!** **NO IN APP PURCHESES!** The game shouldn't just rely on a battle pass or in app purchases like most moble games, IF it becomes a mobile game for some reason Challenges should feel like their own weird little activities instead of standard "solve this puzzle to continue" sections. --- # 6. Divine Points ## What are Divine Points? **Divine Points** are the currency of the game. They are earned by restoring enemys and can be spent on upgrades in the divin path. The full purpose of Divine Points can be expanded as the game develops. ### Possible uses: - Upgrading the divine path - Unlocking story content - Changing parts of the world --- ## Divine Point Progression The player should gradually become more powerful as they collect Divine Points. Early game: > "What are these things? I can use them to upgrade myself?" Mid game: > "I collected alot of divine points! im growing stronger."" Late game: > "I have my dvine path fully unlocked, i can fight the final boss." --- # 7. Upgrades There is a upgrade path used throughout the game called the divine path. The player can spend Divine Points in it. upgrade categories: ### Damage New attacks the player can do. ### Defense lowers damage taken and other ### healing improving how the player heals and can change Some upgrades could have unexpected effects. --- # 8. Items The player can collect different items that can in some ways help progess the story. items aren't always used in battle. Sometimes, they can be used to interact with the environment. Examples: - Keys - Special objects - key items Some items could have multiple uses. --- # 9.
